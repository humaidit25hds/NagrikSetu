# Citizen AI Prototype

The working prototype is implemented as dependency-free HTML wireframes in `../wireframes/`.

## Entry points

- `home-page.html` — landing page, categories, popular services, Senior Citizen Mode
- `services-page.html` — search, filters, service details, official links
- `applications-page.html` — scheme filters, situation checker, document checker
- `chat-page.html` — Setu conversation with suggestions and typed messages
- `user-flows.html` — product journey and service-search explanation
- `help-page.html` and `privacy-page.html` — support and safety content

The shared `nagriksetu.css` and `nagriksetu.js` files keep the screens consistent and make them openable directly in a browser without a build step.
