# NagrikSetu User Personas

## Persona 1: Savita, the First-Time Digital Citizen

**Profile:** 34, homemaker, Maharashtra, uses an Android phone and prefers Marathi or Hindi.

**Goal:** Find out how to access a welfare or identity-related service for her family without travelling to several offices.

**Context:** She knows what outcome she wants, but not the official service name. She is cautious about links and does not want to make a costly mistake.

**Needs:**

- Voice search and regional-language guidance
- A short explanation before any form or portal handoff
- A document checklist with examples
- Official links and scam warnings
- Ability to share instructions with a family member

**Pain points:** Formal government terminology, unclear document requirements, and fear of submitting incorrect information.

**Design response:** Start with intent-based prompts, show one next step at a time, and keep “check official source” visible throughout the flow.

## Persona 2: Ramesh, the Time-Poor Worker

**Profile:** 28, delivery worker, urban, primarily mobile-first, often on a limited data connection.

**Goal:** Quickly identify a useful scheme or service and understand whether it is worth pursuing.

**Context:** He uses short queries, may leave and return later, and needs information he can scan between work tasks.

**Needs:**

- Fast search and category filters
- Compact service cards with the key facts first
- Save or share actions
- Clear time, fee, and document information
- A resumable journey

**Pain points:** Long pages, repeated data entry, unclear status, and dead links.

**Design response:** Put the primary action and important constraints above the fold, preserve saved items, and keep the interface lightweight.

## Persona 3: Meena, the Senior Citizen

**Profile:** 67, pensioner, semi-urban, receives help from her daughter but wants independence.

**Goal:** Understand pension-related information and complete the next step confidently.

**Context:** She may use voice input, has difficulty with small text, and is especially alert to fraud risks.

**Needs:**

- Senior Citizen Mode
- Large, high-contrast text and controls
- Spoken responses with readable transcripts
- Reassurance that Setu will not ask for an OTP or PIN
- Simple back, repeat, and contact-help actions

**Pain points:** Dense screens, unfamiliar icons, fast-changing content, and fear of scams.

**Design response:** Reduce choices, use explicit labels, provide repeatable instructions, and show safety reminders at the point of sharing or applying.

## Persona 4: Anil, the Caregiver Navigator

**Profile:** 41, salaried son and occasional caregiver, supports parents remotely.

**Goal:** Find reliable information and help a family member act without exposing their private details.

**Context:** He compares sources, wants to understand the process end to end, and may share a service guide over messaging apps.

**Needs:**

- Transparent sources and update context
- Shareable step-by-step guidance
- Clear distinction between guidance and official decisions
- Privacy controls and minimal data collection
- A way to switch between services and schemes

**Pain points:** Conflicting online information, inaccessible portals, and unclear responsibility between the assistant and the official department.

**Design response:** Make source provenance, limitations, and handoff points explicit.

## Shared Behavioural Principles

- Users describe goals, not department names.
- Users need confidence before commitment.
- Trust is earned through transparency, not visual polish alone.
- The assistant should clarify, not pressure.
- A successful flow ends with a concrete, verifiable next action.
