# NagrikSetu User Needs

## Product Context

NagrikSetu helps citizens discover government services and schemes through a clear, multilingual, AI-assisted experience. The Setu AI orb is the primary guide, but every important answer must remain understandable and verifiable without trusting the AI blindly.

## Core Needs

### 1. Find the right service quickly

Citizens need a simple way to search and browse services by category, such as identity, certificates, welfare, health, education, and employment. Search results should use plain language and clearly distinguish services from schemes.

### 2. Understand requirements before starting

Each service should explain:

- What the service does
- Who it may be relevant to
- Which documents are commonly needed
- The expected steps
- The official portal or office to use
- Any known fee, time, or application constraints

The product must avoid promising eligibility. Use language such as “may be relevant” and direct users to official sources for confirmation.

### 3. Get help without feeling judged

Users need a conversational assistant that accepts natural questions, voice input, and typed input. The assistant should ask one manageable question at a time, support users with limited digital confidence, and make it easy to correct an answer.

### 4. Trust the information

Users need visible source links, update dates where available, safety reminders, and clear boundaries about what Setu can and cannot do. The experience should warn users never to share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or unnecessary sensitive information.

### 5. Use the product in familiar language

The interface should support regional languages and plain English. Labels, instructions, error states, and assistant responses should avoid bureaucratic language. Voice and readable text are both important for users with literacy, vision, or motor constraints.

### 6. Complete tasks on a mobile connection

The key flow must work on small screens and slower networks. Users should be able to return to a service detail page, save a service, and continue after an interruption without losing context.

### 7. Support older citizens and caregivers

Senior Citizen Mode should offer larger text, stronger contrast, fewer competing actions, and a calmer guided flow. Caregivers should be able to understand the current step without taking over the entire experience.

## Priority Order

1. Correctly identify the user's goal.
2. Explain the next step in plain language.
3. Provide official verification and safety guidance.
4. Reduce document and navigation confusion.
5. Make progress recoverable and shareable.

## Experience Principles

- Clarity before cleverness
- Assistance without false certainty
- One primary action per step
- Visible progress and reversible choices
- Accessibility as a default, not a special mode
