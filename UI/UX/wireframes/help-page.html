Is NagrikSetu a government website?

No. It is an independent citizen assistance platform. Always confirm important details on the official department website.

Does Setu decide my eligibility?

No. Setu can help you explore potentially relevant schemes. The responsible department makes the official decision.

What should I never share?

Never share an OTP, PIN, password, full bank details, or biometric information with an unknown person.

Can I use voice?

Yes. Use the Speak button on the Ask Setu page, or type instead whenever you prefer.

How do I verify a link?

Open official links from the service detail page and check the government domain before entering information.

How does Senior Mode help?

It increases text size and keeps the experience simpler and easier to scan.
