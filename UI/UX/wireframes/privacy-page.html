Privacy and safety

Your trust is part of the service.

NagrikSetu helps you understand public services without asking for more personal information than needed.

Never share these with Setu or an unexpected caller:

OTP codes, passwords, PINs, full bank details, card security codes, or biometric information.

How to use guidance safely

  • Use official links provided in service details.
  • Check the government domain before entering information.
  • Do not pay an unknown person to “unlock” a benefit.
  • Verify changing rules, fees, dates, and eligibility with the responsible department.
  • Share only the minimum information needed for your question.

What Setu does not do

Setu does not represent a government department, approve applications, guarantee eligibility, or replace official verification. It helps you get oriented and take a more informed next step.
